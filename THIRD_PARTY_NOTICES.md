`assets/fonts/xkcd-script.ttf` is bundled to keep custom-font figures self-contained. Its provenance and redistribution terms are not asserted by this repository, so it is excluded from the MIT grant. Confirm the applicable font terms before redistributing it outside this template.

Contributors must document the source and licence of any additional binary asset before adding it.
